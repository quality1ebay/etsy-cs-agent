Privacy Policy

EtsyCsAgent is an internal tool used only by its owner, with the owner's own Google account.

What data it accesses

With the Gmail metadata scope (gmail.metadata), it reads the headers of email messages: sender, recipient, subject and date. It cannot and does not read the body or attachments of any email.

How the data is used

The recipient address of Etsy notification emails is used only to know which of the owner's Etsy shops received a new message. Nothing else is done with the data.

Storage and sharing

The data stays on the owner's own computer and database. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
